1. Apa yang dilakukan aplikasi ini

Aplikasi ini memindai pasar Spot Binance (pair USDT) dengan kerangka Precision Zone System, khusus untuk Spot Long (beli lalu jual lebih tinggi). Tidak ada sinyal short atau sell. Prosesnya:

  1. Menentukan arah trend dengan EMA 50 dan EMA 200.
  2. Mencari zona Supply & Demand (base + impulse) yang masih valid.
  3. Memeriksa apakah harga sedang mendekati atau menyentuh demand zone saat trend UP.
  4. Mencari konfirmasi candle (pin bar atau engulfing) di zona, ditambah konteks SMC/price action (BOS, CHoCH, liquidity sweep).
  5. Menyusun tiket eksekusi Binance Spot: Limit/Market Buy lalu OCO Sell (TP + SL). Harga dan jumlah koin sudah dibulatkan sesuai aturan pair di Binance.
  6. Menyimpan rencana ke Jurnal untuk melacak hasil (R, win rate, P&L).
Datanya dari API publik Binance, jadi tidak perlu login atau API key, dan aplikasi tidak bisa menaruh order. Semua perhitungan dilakukan di browser Anda. Aturan di sini adalah terjemahan algoritmik dari konsep Precision Zone (EMA 50/200 + S&D). Angka-angkanya bisa disesuaikan dengan cheat sheet Anda di tab Pengaturan.

4. Aturan sistem (logika di balik sinyal)

4.1 Trend (filter utama)

KondisiTrendYang dicari
EMA50 > EMA200 dan harga > EMA200UPCari BUY di Demand zone
EMA50 < EMA200 dan harga < EMA200DOWNHindari beli. Kalau sedang memegang koinnya, pertimbangkan ambil profit atau keluar.
Selain ituSIDEWAYSTunggu (tidak ada sinyal)

Pullback ke area di antara EMA50 dan EMA200 saat trend UP sering menjadi lokasi demand zone dengan probabilitas terbaik.

4.2 Zona Supply & Demand

  • Base: 1–3 candle kecil (body ≤ 50% range atau ≤ 0,5×ATR), yaitu area konsolidasi.
  • Impulse (leg-out): candle besar setelah base, dengan body ≥ 1,2×ATR (bisa diatur) dan body ≥ 55% range. Penutupannya harus keluar jauh dari base.
  • Proximal line: batas zona yang dekat dengan harga (body tertinggi base untuk demand). Ini level entry limit.
  • Distal line: batas zona yang jauh (low terendah base untuk demand). Stop loss diletakkan di bawahnya.
  • Pola demand: DBR (Drop-Base-Rally) adalah zona reversal, biasanya lebih kuat. RBR (Rally-Base-Rally) adalah zona continuation.
  • Supply zone (RBD/DBD) tetap digambar di chart. Untuk Spot Long, fungsinya sebagai target TP dan peringatan: kalau supply terlalu dekat di atas harga, ruang naiknya sempit.

4.3 Status zona

StatusArtiKualitas
FreshBelum pernah disentuh lagi sejak terbentukTerbaik
Tested 1×Sudah disentuh satu kaliCukup
LemahSudah disentuh 2× atau lebih (order sudah terserap)Hindari
BrokenCandle menutup melewati distal line, zona tidak berlakuDihapus otomatis

4.4 Konfirmasi candle (di zona)

  • Pin bar / rejection: wick ke arah zona ≥ 2× body dan ≥ 50% range, close di setengah range yang berlawanan.
  • Engulfing: body candle terakhir menelan body candle sebelumnya, ke arah sinyal.

4.5 Konteks SMC / price action

  • Swing high/low: pivot dengan 3 candle di kiri dan kanan.
  • BOS (Break of Structure): close menembus swing searah bias. Artinya trend berlanjut.
  • CHoCH (Change of Character): close menembus swing berlawanan bias. Ini tanda awal pembalikan.
  • Liquidity sweep: wick menembus swing low/high tetapi close kembali ke dalam. Stop loss ritel tersapu, sehingga sering mendahului pergerakan ke arah sebaliknya.

7. Cara eksekusi di Binance Spot (Tiket Eksekusi)

Tiket eksekusi mengubah sinyal menjadi order yang tinggal disalin ke Binance. Harga dibulatkan ke tick size dan jumlah koin ke step size pair tersebut, lalu dicek terhadap nilai order minimum Binance.

Langkah 1 · Beli

  • Siap eksekusi: Binance → Spot → pair → Buy → Market, isi Total USDT dari tiket (atau Limit di harga sekarang).
  • Siapkan limit: Buy → Limit, isi Harga limit dan Jumlah dari tiket. Order menunggu sampai harga turun ke zona.

Langkah 2 (& 3) · Pasang OCO Sell setelah order beli terisi

  1. Pilih tab Sell → OCO.
  2. Take profit / Limit = harga TP dari tiket.
  3. Stop = harga Stop (trigger), dan Stop-limit = harga Stop-limit (sedikit di bawah stop supaya order jual pasti tereksekusi saat harga jatuh cepat).
  4. Jumlah = qty di tiket. Jumlah ini sudah dikurangi fee 0,1% yang dipotong dalam koin.
  5. Kalau memilih 50% TP1 · 50% TP2, buat 2 OCO (A dan B) dengan stop yang sama.

Setelah TP1 tercapai

OCO A selesai. Batalkan OCO B lalu pasang ulang dengan stop di harga entry (break-even), supaya sisa posisi bebas risiko.

Kalau ada peringatan merah (di bawah nilai minimum order), perbesar modal atau % posisi, atau pilih 100% di TP1. Kalau bayar fee pakai BNB, jumlah koin yang bisa dijual sedikit lebih besar dari tiket, dan itu tidak masalah.

9. Checklist sebelum entry
  1. Trend timeframe utama UP dan HTF tidak DOWN.
  2. Demand zone Fresh atau Tested 1×, bukan Lemah.
  3. Tidak ada supply zone yang terlalu dekat di atas harga.
  4. Impulse yang keluar dari zona kuat (≥ 1,5×ATR lebih baik).
  5. Ada candle konfirmasi di zona (pin bar atau engulfing).
  6. Ada konteks SMC pendukung: BOS/CHoCH ke atas atau sweep swing low.
  7. RR ke TP1 ≥ Min RR (default 2).
  8. Tidak ada berita besar (FOMC, CPI, unlock token) dalam beberapa jam ke depan.
  9. Ukuran posisi sudah dihitung sesuai risiko %.

1. What this app does

This app scans the Binance Spot market (USDT pairs) using the Precision Zone System framework, for Spot Long trades only (buy, then sell higher). It never gives short or sell signals. The process:

  1. Determine the trend direction with EMA 50 and EMA 200.
  2. Find Supply & Demand zones (base + impulse) that are still valid.
  3. Check whether price is approaching or tapping a demand zone while the trend is UP.
  4. Look for a confirmation candle (pin bar or engulfing) at the zone, plus SMC/price-action context (BOS, CHoCH, liquidity sweep).
  5. Build a Binance Spot execution ticket: Limit/Market Buy followed by an OCO Sell (TP + SL). Prices and quantities are already rounded to the pair's Binance rules.
  6. Save the plan to the Journal to track results (R, win rate, P&L).
Data comes from Binance's public API, so no login or API key is needed, and the app cannot place orders. All calculations run in your browser. The rules here are an algorithmic translation of the Precision Zone concept (EMA 50/200 + S&D). You can tune the numbers to match your cheat sheet in the Settings tab.

4. System rules (the logic behind the signals)

4.1 Trend (main filter)

ConditionTrendWhat to look for
EMA50 > EMA200 and price > EMA200UPLook for BUYs at demand zones
EMA50 < EMA200 and price < EMA200DOWNAvoid buying. If you already hold the coin, consider taking profit or exiting.
Anything elseSIDEWAYSWait (no signal)

In an uptrend, pullbacks into the area between EMA50 and EMA200 are often where the highest-probability demand zones sit.

4.2 Supply & Demand zones

  • Base: 1–3 small candles (body ≤ 50% of range or ≤ 0.5×ATR), i.e. a consolidation area.
  • Impulse (leg-out): a large candle after the base, with body ≥ 1.2×ATR (adjustable) and body ≥ 55% of range. It must close well away from the base.
  • Proximal line: the zone boundary closest to price (the highest body of the base for demand). This is the limit entry level.
  • Distal line: the far boundary (the lowest low of the base for demand). The stop loss goes below it.
  • Demand patterns: DBR (Drop-Base-Rally) is a reversal zone and usually stronger. RBR (Rally-Base-Rally) is a continuation zone.
  • Supply zones (RBD/DBD) are still drawn on the chart. For spot longs they serve as TP targets and as a warning: if supply sits too close above price, upside room is limited.

4.3 Zone status

StatusMeaningQuality
FreshNot revisited since it formedBest
Tested 1×Revisited onceAcceptable
WeakRevisited 2× or more (orders already absorbed)Avoid
BrokenA candle closed beyond the distal line, so the zone is invalidRemoved automatically

4.4 Confirmation candle (at the zone)

  • Pin bar / rejection: wick toward the zone ≥ 2× the body and ≥ 50% of range, closing in the opposite half of the range.
  • Engulfing: the last candle's body engulfs the previous candle's body, in the signal direction.

4.5 SMC / price-action context

  • Swing high/low: a pivot with 3 candles on each side.
  • BOS (Break of Structure): a close beyond a swing in the direction of the bias, meaning the trend continues.
  • CHoCH (Change of Character): a close beyond a swing against the bias, an early sign of reversal.
  • Liquidity sweep: a wick beyond a swing low/high that closes back inside. Retail stop losses get swept, which often precedes a move in the opposite direction.

7. Executing on Binance Spot (Execution Ticket)

The execution ticket turns a signal into orders you can copy straight into Binance. Prices are rounded to the pair's tick size and quantities to its step size, then checked against Binance's minimum order value.

Step 1 · Buy

  • Ready to execute: Binance → Spot → pair → Buy → Market, enter the Total USDT from the ticket (or a Limit at the current price).
  • Set limit order: Buy → Limit, enter the Limit price and Quantity from the ticket. The order waits until price drops into the zone.

Step 2 (& 3) · Place the OCO Sell once the buy is filled

  1. Choose the Sell → OCO tab.
  2. Take profit / Limit = the TP price from the ticket.
  3. Stop = the Stop (trigger) price, and Stop-limit = the Stop-limit price (slightly below the stop so the sell order fills even when price drops fast).
  4. Quantity = the qty on the ticket. It already accounts for the 0.1% fee deducted in coins.
  5. If you chose 50% TP1 · 50% TP2, create 2 OCOs (A and B) with the same stop.

After TP1 is hit

OCO A is done. Cancel OCO B and re-place it with the stop at your entry price (break-even), so the remaining position is risk-free.

If you see a red warning (below the minimum order value), increase your capital or position %, or choose 100% at TP1. If you pay fees in BNB, you'll be able to sell slightly more coins than the ticket shows, which is fine.
8. Trading plan & position management
LevelRule (Spot Long)
EntryConfirmed: last close (market). Setup: limit buy at the demand proximal line.
Stop LossDemand distal − 0.2×ATR. On Spot, place it as a stop-limit or OCO.
TP1The proximal line of the nearest supply zone above entry, if it is ≥ 1R away. Otherwise Entry + (Min RR × risk).
TP2Entry + (Min RR + 1) × risk, at least 3R

Recommended execution rules

  1. Risk at most 1–2% of capital per trade. Use the calculator on the details page.
  2. The calculator has 2 position sizing methods:
    • By risk %: you set what % of capital you are willing to lose if the SL is hit, and the app calculates the quantity. The position can be large when the SL is tight.
    • By % of capital: you choose the position size (5%–100% of capital or custom), and the app calculates the loss if the SL is hit. A warning appears if risk exceeds 2% of capital.
    The default method and percentage are set in the Settings tab.
  3. At TP1, close about 50% of the position and move the SL to break-even.
  4. Let the rest run to TP2, or trail it below the latest swing low.
  5. If a candle closes below the distal line, exit. The zone has failed.
  6. On Binance Spot, use an OCO (One-Cancels-the-Other) order so TP and SL are placed together.
  7. Avoid entries where the TP1 RR is below 1.5, or the SL is more than 3×ATR.
9. Pre-entry checklist
  1. Main timeframe trend is UP and the HTF is not DOWN.
  2. Demand zone is Fresh or Tested 1×, not Weak.
  3. No supply zone sitting too close above price.
  4. The impulse leaving the zone is strong (≥ 1.5×ATR is better).
  5. A confirmation candle printed at the zone (pin bar or engulfing).
  6. Supporting SMC context: an upward BOS/CHoCH or a swing-low sweep.
  7. RR to TP1 ≥ Min RR (default 2).
  8. No major news (FOMC, CPI, token unlocks) in the next few hours.
  9. Position size calculated according to your risk %.

The details page shows an automatic checklist for points 1–7. Check points 8–9 yourself.

10. Watchlist & trading journal

Watchlist

  • Tap ☆ on a card or the details page to save a pair. You can also add pairs manually in the Watchlist tab.
  • Scan watchlist analyses only your favourite pairs, so it is faster. Good for checking every few hours.

Journal

  1. In the Execution Ticket press ＋ Save to journal. The initial status is Awaiting entry (limit) or Position open (market).
  2. Once the limit is filled, press ✓ Order filled.
  3. Close the position with Hit TP1, Hit SL, or enter an exit price and press Close position.
  4. Press ↻ Refresh prices to see running R and alerts (price at entry, TP1 reached, price ≤ SL).

Statistics calculate win rate, average R, total R and total P&L from closed trades. R = result divided by initial risk: +2R means a profit of 2× your risk, −1R means a full stop-out.

Journal and watchlist data are stored in this browser. Use Settings → Backup data regularly, then Restore on another browser or phone.

14. Glossary
ATRAverage True Range: the average distance price moves per candle. Used to measure distances relative to volatility.
Demand zoneA price area where large buyers previously stepped in (the base before a strong rally). A buy area.
Supply zoneAn area where large sellers stepped in. In this app it is used as a TP target.
Proximal / DistalThe zone boundary nearest to price (entry level) and the far boundary (SL basis).
FreshA zone that hasn't been revisited, so its orders are still intact.
HTFHigher Timeframe, the timeframe above (e.g. 1D for a 4H scan).
BOS / CHoCHBreak of Structure (trend continuation) and Change of Character (early reversal sign).
Liquidity sweepA wick through a swing low that closes back above it, a sign that retail stop losses were swept.
RRRisk:Reward, potential profit divided by potential loss. RR 2 means a profit of 2× the risk.
RA trade's result relative to its initial risk (+1R = profit equal to the risk).
OCOOne-Cancels-the-Other: two sell orders (TP and SL) at once. When one fills, the other is cancelled automatically.
Tick / Step sizeThe smallest price and quantity increments Binance allows for that pair.
